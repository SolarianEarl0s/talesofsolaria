Act I · Book 1

Evin's Tale

A soul of fire. A world of secrets.

Released 28 September 2026 · Free

Everyone is born able to cast magic. The nobles have spent centuries making sure no one remembers that. Evin is a street thief turned palace servant. Invisible, lowborn, and certain magic is a birthright he'll never have. Then a forbidden melody draws him into a room he was never meant to enter, and the lie that built his entire world begins to come apart in his hands.

You play as
Evin, in the Kingdom of Calbra
Plays in
Your browser, on desktop or phone (on-screen pad for touch)
Controls

    Act II · Alara's Story

    Two Flames

    Grief ignites. Power awakens. Worlds break.

    Beta Free · Plays in your browser

    A brother and a sister, separated by one snowy morning, each sure the other is dead. Walk Oōdengard's road and Alara's road in either order. Neither of them knows the whole story. By the end, you will.

    Oōdengard and Alara, one road each

      Act II·V · Interlude

      The Age of Pirates

      Why are the pirates preparing, and for what?

      Beta Version 24 · Free · Plays in your browser

      The pirates used to wave at Frontier ships. Now they fire on them, and every kingdom on the coast is building a fleet. Grand Elder Alara sends two of her own to find out why: Chili, who can talk to anyone, and Shadow Cloak, who notices everything.

      You play as
      Chili and Shadow Cloak, swapping between them, or one each with a friend. A third player joins as Cadet Jory, the healer.
      Multiplayer
      Up to three players on one keyboard, or online: one player hosts and the others join with a room code
      Side games
      Darts and blackjack at the Salt & Sun
      Modes
      The Rookie & The Expert: playable, both routes. The Pirate Captain: in progress.
      Controls
      ArrowsmoveZattack / talkXrollCskillVswapEeatEscjournal
      Also works with
      Touch screens and game controllers
